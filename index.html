THE WORKSHOP

A new era of fashion,
built by young talent.

NOVERA '26 is an exclusive two-day Modeling & Personality Development Workshop organized by AIESEC in CINEC — built to empower aspiring youth, models, and fashion enthusiasts with the skills, confidence, and industry exposure needed to step into the professional modeling and creative world.

AIESEC in CINEC exists to cultivate professional skill sets, soft skills, and leadership qualities in university students. NOVERA is our launchpad for undergraduates who want to excel beyond the classroom — this time, on the runway.

Why "NOVERA"?

Three ideas behind the name, and the experience we're building around them.

“

A new era of fashion

Redefining traditional modeling by focusing on authenticity, versatility, and modern creative aesthetics.

“

Empowering youth talent

“

Direct brand engagement

Creating a space where premier beauty, fashion, and lifestyle brands connect directly with an energetic youth audience.
